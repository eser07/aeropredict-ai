P7.4 · What the saved evidence can (and cannot) verify

These are independent data-quality observations, not a confidence percentage, investment score or current market health check.

USD prices: Complete means the saved reward USD amounts were covered by collection-time quotes. Missing prices are Unknown, not $0. Coverage does not establish token liquidity.

Quote timestamps: Some reward-token quotes have saved UTC timestamps; their presence and range can be inspected per pool. A missing timestamp does not prove a missing price, and a timestamp does not make the quote live.

Four-week evidence: Positive FEES/BRIBES observations cover four completed weeks only. Zero weeks are observations, not predictions. Truncated registry markers remain visible.

Independent P4 votes: The inventory's older block and individually pinned P4 vote blocks/times are not synchronized with weekly rewards or across all pools.

How the 0–100 historical research score works

0–100 research score: 30 points for observed reward weeks (4 weeks), up to 25 for saved-quote weekly USD (logarithmic $10–$1,000), 25 for complete USD coverage, and up to 20 for old vote context (logarithmic 100–10,000 veAERO). Fragile ratios (less than 100 old votes, unknown votes or less than $10 weekly USD) receive graded evidence discounts and are capped at 39; this is a heuristic, not a probability. Incomplete USD data is unrated, not zero. Scores do not predict APR, veNFT earnings, market liquidity, risk or future rewards. No cross-epoch ranking.
