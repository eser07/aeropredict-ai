Important limitations

Historical epoch keys were queried against the latest Base contract state. These are not immutable historical block snapshots. Reward allocations are not personal veAERO earnings, APR, claimable balances, or guaranteed payments.

Twelve weeks of observations provide only eight walk-forward test periods per series. These are simple statistical baselines, not a validated AI forecasting system.
